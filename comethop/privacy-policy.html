Comet Hop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 (version 1.3)

This policy covers the Android game Comet Hop (package app.comethop.game), published on Google Play by the developer TeRakau ("we").

What we collect

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information ourselves. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no servers of our own. The ads in the free version are provided by Google AdMob, which collects data as described under "Advertising" below.

Your progress

Your best score, checkpoint, stars, unlocked skins, missions and settings are saved on your device.

Google Play Games: if you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game also stores a copy of your progress (best score, checkpoint, stars, unlocked skins, completed missions and play counts) in your Google Play Games Saved Games, so it follows you to a new phone. Google Play Games also shares your Play Games player ID and gamer name with the game so it can show that you are signed in. This data is stored and processed by Google under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); we do not run servers and never receive it. You can delete it at any time from your Google Play Games settings. Energy and sound/vibration settings stay on the device only.

In-app purchase

The optional "Unlock Full Game" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether the unlock has been bought, so it can restore it on a new device or after a reinstall. Google's privacy policy explains how Google handles purchases: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising

The free version of Comet Hop shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some games, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward. AdMob, a Google service, may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and app interaction and diagnostic data to serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time with the PRIVACY button on the main menu. Anyone can reset or delete their advertising ID in Android settings. Buying the full game turns all ads off. How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Permissions

  • Vibration: haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game.
  • Internet, network state and Google Play billing: used only by Google Play to process and restore the in-app purchase.
  • Advertising ID: used only by Google AdMob to show ads in the free version.
  • Some background-task permissions are added automatically by Google's libraries. The game does not use them to collect data.

Children

Comet Hop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions or requests: email the developer, TeRakau, at the contact address shown on Comet Hop's Google Play listing.
